In the holy city of Pannelion, a civilisation where blade and sorcery became one, stood a glorious bastion and a warm home to its people. Silver-armoured knights, wizards and witches lived together in peace beneath a protective veil for a thousand years. The long peace made everyone believe the calm would last forever.

Until... the veil that had guarded the city began to crack. A once-bright sky was stained blood red by a calamity no one foresaw. Death crept up to the city walls, led by hordes of maddened goblins pouring in until they blotted out the sky, and cursed stone golems that crushed everything without mercy, all pulled by the strings of a dark witch watching from the shadows. And above every fear loomed a terror in the heavens... the Dragon of Ruin, newly woken from its long slumber.

This deranged army has a single goal: to raze the city walls and seize the last breath of magic left in this world. There are no reinforcements and nowhere left to retreat. The time has come for the knights to awaken their guardian power and their trusted weapons to clear the way, and for the archmages to unleash the highest elemental magic to sweep away the enemy. Every soul must rise and stand firm, giving sweat and blood to reclaim and protect Pannelion until a new dawn arrives!
